About

smAisle is a one-person game studio in New York City, built by me, Jeff. The idea was simple: make games anyone could play. Each one starts out as an idea at home. We work out the rules as a family, then everyone tests and tweaks it, from my kids to the grandparents, until it's easy to pick up.

I use Ai to help build them, which is how a tiny studio can make so many. That's the "Ai" in smAisle. No ads, no accounts, no tricks: just games you can learn in a minute.
