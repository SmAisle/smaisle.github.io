Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

This policy covers every game made by smAisle, an independent developer: the iPhone apps on the App Store, including smAisle Daily, and the free versions you can play in a web browser at smaisle.app. The games include smAisle Daily, The Daily 15, A, B, C, Deal, Gramble, Jexicon, Capital Ship, Blipword, Straight Down, Bullseye Roulette, Shuffle Blast and Connello.

The short version: no accounts, no ads, no tracking across apps or websites, and nothing is ever sold. Your scores, streaks and settings stay on your device. Some games send us an anonymous usage count so we know how many people play. It never includes your name, email, contacts or location.

What we collect

Anonymous usage counts

To count how many people play and which games they like, some games send a small check-in, usually once a day when you open the game and again when you finish the day's puzzle. A check-in can include:

  • a random ID made up by the game on your device. It isn't linked to you, your Apple Account, your phone number or your device's advertising ID;
  • the game version, and whether you're on a phone or a large screen;
  • the dates you played, how many times you opened the game, and which games you tapped on the smAisle Daily home screen;
  • game results such as puzzles finished, words solved, scores, streaks and modes played;
  • for the website versions that ask you to agree to terms first, the date you agreed.

A check-in never includes your name, email, contacts, location, photos or anything you type. We don't use check-ins for advertising, don't share them, and don't combine them with other information to identify you. On the App Store, this appears as Identifiers, Usage Data and Gameplay Content, Not Linked to You.

Which games send check-ins

  • smAisle Daily iPhone app: the home screen and The Daily 15 send check-ins. The other daily games inside the app don't.
  • Website versions: smAisle Daily, The Daily 15, A, B, C, Deal, Gramble, Jexicon, Capital Ship, Blipword, Straight Down, Shuffle Blast, Bullseye Roulette and Connello send check-ins. Straight Down, Shuffle Blast, Bullseye Roulette and Connello ask you to agree first.
  • Straight Down iPhone app: nothing. It doesn't collect, store or send any information to smAisle.

Invite codes

While some games are in testing, the website asks for an invite code. When you enter one, the website records the code, a random device ID, and which testing games that device opened, so we know how many testers each code brings. It doesn't record who you are.

Leaderboards

Some games let you post a score to a public leaderboard. If you choose to, the nickname you type and your score are shown to everyone. Please don't use your full real name if you'd rather stay private.

Stored on your device

Your puzzles, results, streaks, settings (such as dark mode) and player initials are saved on your device, in the app or in your browser, so the game remembers them next time. Deleting the app or clearing the website's data in your browser deletes them.

iCloud

The smAisle Daily app can keep your results and streaks in step across your own Apple devices using Apple's iCloud. That data goes to your iCloud account, not to smAisle, and we can't see it.

Apple services

Game Center

If you're signed in to Apple's Game Center, some apps can post your scores to Game Center leaderboards, where other players see them next to your Game Center nickname. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. smAisle sees only the leaderboard entries, as any player can. You can turn Game Center off in your iPhone's Settings.

In-app purchases

Purchases, such as smAisle Daily Plus or unlocking multiplayer, are handled entirely by Apple. smAisle never sees your name, payment details or Apple Account. The app only learns whether the purchase has been made.

Service providers

Check-ins, invite records and leaderboard entries are stored with Google Firebase, a cloud database service. Only smAisle can read check-ins and invite records. The website is hosted by GitHub Pages, and some pages load fonts from Google Fonts. Like most websites, these services may receive your IP address and browser details when you load a page. See GitHub's privacy statement and Google's privacy policy. The iPhone apps include their own copies of the fonts.

Children

The games are family friendly, with no ads, no chat and no way to message other players. They aren't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has posted personal information as a leaderboard nickname, email us and we'll remove it.

Your choices

  • On your device: delete the app, or clear the website's data in your browser settings. This also resets the random ID.
  • Leaderboard entries: email us your nickname, the game, the score and roughly when you posted it, and we'll delete it.
  • Check-ins: because they aren't linked to you, we usually can't tell which ones are yours. If you can tell us the game and roughly when you played, we'll do our best to find and delete them.

We keep check-ins only as long as they're useful for counting players, and invite records only while the games are in testing.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top. If a change affects what an app collects, we'll update its App Store listing too.
