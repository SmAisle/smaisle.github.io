Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026

This policy covers the games Straight Down, Connello and The Daily 15, made by smAisle, an independent developer. It applies to the iPhone apps on the App Store and to the free versions you can play in a web browser at smaisle.github.io.

The short version: no accounts, no ads, no tracking, and nothing is ever sold. Your scores and settings stay on your device. The only thing any game sends us is an anonymous usage count (explained below), and the Straight Down and Connello iPhone apps don't send even that.

iPhone apps

Information we collect

Straight Down and Connello: none. These apps do not collect, store or send any personal information to smAisle. They contain no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking.

The Daily 15: an anonymous usage count, so we know how many people play. Once a day when you open the app, and again when you finish the daily puzzle, it sends a random ID created by the app (not linked to you, your Apple Account or your device's advertising ID), the app version, the days you played in the last 60 days and how many words you placed each day, how many daily puzzles you've finished, and whether you played the demo. It never includes your name, contacts, location or anything you type. It isn't shared with anyone, isn't used for ads, and isn't used to track you across other apps or websites. The App Store lists this as Usage Data and Identifiers, not linked to you.

Stored on your device

Your settings, best scores, player initials and game history are saved only on your iPhone, so the game remembers them next time. Deleting the app deletes them.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Apple's Game Center, your scores can be posted to the game's leaderboards, where other players see them next to your Game Center nickname. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. smAisle can see the leaderboard entries, as any player can, but receives no other information about you. You can turn Game Center off in your iPhone's Settings.

In-app purchases

Purchases, such as unlocking multiplayer, are handled entirely by Apple. smAisle never sees your name, payment details or Apple Account; the app only learns whether the unlock has been bought.

Website versions

The Daily 15

The browser version sends the same anonymous usage count as the app (see above), plus whether you're on a large screen. Your puzzles, streak and settings are saved in your browser on your device.

Connello

The browser version of Connello saves your settings and initials in your browser on your device. It doesn't send any game data anywhere.

Straight Down

The browser version of Straight Down asks you to agree to its terms before you play. After you agree:

  • Daily check-in. Once a day, the game sends an anonymous check-in so we can count how many devices play. It includes a random ID created by the game (not linked to you), the game version, how many games you've played and in which modes, your best score, your speed setting, and the date you accepted the terms. It never includes your name, contacts or location.
  • Leaderboard. If you choose to post a score, the nickname you type and your score are shown publicly on the leaderboard. Please don't use your full real name if you'd rather stay private.

Check-ins from all the games are stored with Google Firebase, a cloud database service. Only smAisle can read them.

Hosting and fonts

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages, and its fonts load from Google Fonts. Like most websites, these services may receive your IP address and browser details when you load a page. See GitHub's privacy statement and Google's privacy policy.

Children

The games contain no ads, no chat and no way to contact other players. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has posted personal information as a leaderboard nickname, email us and we'll remove it.

Deleting your information

  • On your device: delete the app, or clear the website's data in your browser settings.
  • Leaderboard entries or check-ins: email smaisle.app@gmail.com with your nickname, the score and roughly when you posted it, and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top. If a change affects what the apps collect, we'll update the app's App Store listing too.

Contact

Questions about privacy: smaisle.app@gmail.com
